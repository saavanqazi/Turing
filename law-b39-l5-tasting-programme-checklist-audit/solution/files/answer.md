# The tasting checklist against the regulation and the guide

Checklist items the draft got wrong: 7

The checklist was built from the association guide, so on every subject the regulation also covers, the guide gives way. The brand's state permit, the store's own permit and age verification are required as the draft says, under BR-201, BR-203 and BR-207. Where the regulation says nothing the guide governs, so the sample size limit and the tasting log are required under AG-305 and AG-306, and the post-event report is required under AG-310 although the draft treats it as unnecessary.

Most of the draft's errors turn on a single fact it never took account of: the people working the table are the agency's own staff, not employees of the brand that holds the permit. BR-202 therefore requires each of them to hold a solicitor permit, whatever guide note AG-302 has long said. BR-206 excuses a brand representative only where an employee of the permit holder conducts the tasting, so a representative is required here and the draft is right to list one. BR-208 posts the prescribed notice unless every server is such an employee, and that exception does not reach these tastings, so the notice is required. AG-307 excuses a training certificate only for the permit holder's own servers and expects agency staff to hold one.

BR-205 runs the other way. Because each store holds a tasting permit in its own name under BR-203, that permit stands as its consent and no written consent of the owner need be filed, so the draft is wrong to call for one. The draft is wrong again on the separate permit per store, which BR-209 does not issue, and on the advance notice, which BR-204 requires.

The identification card, the insurance certificate and the ordinance review cite sections that do not exist and concern subjects neither document addresses, so nothing governs them and they are neither right nor wrong.
