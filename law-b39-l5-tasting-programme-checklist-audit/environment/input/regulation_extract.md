# Beverage-control regulation — tastings at retail (extract)

The sections of the regulation that bear on a tasting programme. Each section states one position; the tag names the subject the checklist's `subject` column refers to.

## BR-201 `brand_state_permit`

A brand whose products are offered at a tasting holds a state tasting permit for the tasting period.

## BR-202 `agency_staff_solicitor_permit`

Any person who conducts a tasting and is not an employee of the permit holder holds a solicitor permit of their own; the permit holder's permit covers its own employees and nobody else.

## BR-203 `store_tasting_permit`

A retail store at which a tasting is held holds its own tasting permit.

## BR-204 `advance_notice_filing`

Notice of each tasting is filed with the division before the tasting day.

## BR-205 `store_owner_written_consent`

No written consent of the store owner need be filed or kept; the store's own permit is its consent.

## BR-206 `brand_representative_present`

No representative of the brand need be present at a tasting.

## BR-207 `age_verification`

The age of every person served is verified before service.

## BR-208 `signage_posted`

A notice in the prescribed form is posted at the tasting table.

## BR-209 `permit_per_store`

One tasting permit covers every store at which the holder offers tastings in the permit period; no separate permit per store is issued.

## BR-210 `tasting_hours`

Tastings are held within the hours the permit states.

## BR-211 `sealed_containers`

Product for a tasting arrives in sealed containers opened at the table.
