# Source register — tastings at retail

Every record the programme holds. A record carries the licensing district it
applies in, the subject it speaks to, and the date it took effect; the holding is
what the record holds. `BR-` records are the beverage-control regulation, `AG-`
records the trade association's guide.

## Regulation

### BR-201 — D-ARD, 2026-01-05 — subject `brand_permit`

A state tasting permit held by the brand is required.

### BR-207 — D-BEL, 2026-01-12 — subject `age_verification`

The age of every person served verified before service is required.

### BR-210 — D-DUN, 2026-01-12 — subject `tasting_hours`

The tasting held within the hours the permit states is required.

### BR-203 — D-BEL, 2026-01-19 — subject `store_permit`

A tasting permit held by the store in its own name is required.

### BR-204 — D-BEL, 2026-01-19 — subject `advance_notice`

Notice of the tasting filed with the division beforehand is required.

### BR-202 — D-ARD, 2026-02-02 — subject `server_permit`

Where `staffed_by_agency` is `yes`, a solicitor permit held by each person serving is required; otherwise it is not required.

### BR-205 — D-COR, 2026-02-09 — subject `owner_consent`

Where `store_permit_on_file` is `no`, the store owner's written consent on file is required; otherwise it is not required.

### BR-209 — D-COR, 2026-02-09 — subject `permit_per_store`

A separate tasting permit for each store is not required.

### BR-206 — D-ARD, 2026-02-16 — subject `brand_rep_present`

A representative of the brand present at the table is not required.

### BR-208 — D-BEL, 2026-02-23 — subject `signage`

Where `all_servers_employees` is `no`, a notice in the prescribed form posted at the tasting table is required; otherwise it is not required.

### BR-211 — D-COR, 2026-03-09 — subject `sealed_containers`

Product arriving in sealed containers opened at the table is required.

### BR-216 — D-ARD, 2026-03-30 — subject `brand_rep_present`

Where `staffed_by_agency` is `yes`, a representative of the brand present at the table is required; otherwise it is not required.

### BR-217 — D-DUN, 2026-04-20 — subject `server_training`

A current training certificate held by each server is not required.

### BR-218 — D-BEL, 2026-04-27 — subject `store_permit`

A tasting permit held by the store in its own name is not required.

## Association guide

### AG-301 — D-ARD, 2025-11-10 — subject `brand_permit`

A state tasting permit held by the brand is required.

### AG-305 — D-COR, 2026-01-26 — subject `sample_size`

Where `sample_over_limit` is `yes`, each sample kept within the prescribed size is required; otherwise it is not required.

### AG-306 — D-COR, 2026-01-26 — subject `tasting_log`

A log of each tasting kept by the holder is required.

### AG-302 — D-ARD, 2026-02-02 — subject `server_permit`

A solicitor permit held by each person serving is not required.

### AG-307 — D-DUN, 2026-02-02 — subject `server_training`

Where `staffed_by_agency` is `yes`, a current training certificate held by each server is required; otherwise it is not required.

### AG-311 — D-ARD, 2026-02-16 — subject `tasting_hours`

The tasting held within the hours the permit states is required.

### AG-310 — D-DUN, 2026-03-02 — subject `post_event_report`

A report filed with the division after the event is required.

### AG-304 — D-BEL, 2026-03-16 — subject `advance_notice`

Notice of the tasting filed with the division beforehand is not required.

### AG-308 — D-BEL, 2026-04-13 — subject `signage`

A notice in the prescribed form posted at the tasting table is not required.

### AG-312 — D-DUN, 2026-05-18 — subject `insurance_filed`

A certificate of insurance filed with the division is required.
