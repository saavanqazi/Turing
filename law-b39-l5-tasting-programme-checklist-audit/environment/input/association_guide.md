# Association guide — running in-store tastings (trade precedent)

The trade association's notes for agencies. Each note carries the subject the checklist's `subject` column refers to; a subject with no note here is one the guide does not address.

## AG-301 `brand_state_permit`

The brand needs a state tasting permit covering the dates.

## AG-302 `agency_staff_solicitor_permit`

Agency staff at the table work under the brand's permit and need no permit of their own; this has been the practice for years.

## AG-303 `store_tasting_permit`

Check that the store holds its own tasting permit.

## AG-304 `advance_notice_filing`

File notice of the event with the division ahead of time.

## AG-305 `sample_size_limit`

Keep every sample within the size limit.

## AG-306 `tasting_log`

Keep a log of each tasting: date, store, product, servings.

## AG-307 `server_training_certificate`

Servers need no training certificate for a tasting.

## AG-308 `brand_representative_present`

A brand representative need not attend.

## AG-309 `age_verification`

Verify every taster's age before pouring.

## AG-310 `post_event_report`

File a short report after each event.

## AG-311 `tasting_hours`

Stay within the hours on the permit.
