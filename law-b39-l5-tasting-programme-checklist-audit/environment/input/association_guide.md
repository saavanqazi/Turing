# Association guide — running in-store tastings (trade precedent)

The trade association's notes for agencies.

## AG-301

The brand needs a state tasting permit covering the dates.

## AG-302

Agency staff at the table work under the brand's permit and need no permit of their own.

## AG-303

Check that the store holds its own tasting permit.

## AG-304

File notice of the event with the division ahead of time.

## AG-305

Keep every sample within the size limit.

## AG-306

Keep a log of each tasting: date, store, product, servings.

## AG-307

Servers employed by the permit holder need no training certificate. Agency staff at the table hold a current certificate.

## AG-308

A brand representative need not attend.

## AG-309

Verify every taster's age before pouring.

## AG-310

File a short report after each event.

## AG-311

Stay within the hours on the permit.
